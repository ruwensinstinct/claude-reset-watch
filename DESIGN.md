# Reset Watch design

A compact editorial observation station, not a SaaS dashboard. Main mode: reading and checking evidence. Dark ink masthead, vivid warm signal orange, pale green-paper archive. Barlow Condensed display lettering gives an economical, distinctive press-like rhythm; ordinary sans-serif body copy supports longer explanations. Thin rules and aligned rows, no nested cards, no decorative metrics, no probability dial, no gradient or glass effects.

The first viewport answers two different questions: a publicly announced reset exists; another reset today is not predictable from current evidence. Eligibility and individual-account uncertainty stay next to the claim, not buried in a footer. The historical archive visibly distinguishes primary confirmations from secondary hints.

Read and applied public Impeccable guidance: the project README, skill design vocabulary, new-work reference, and craft-floor reference from https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable and https://impeccable.style . Used as design references only. No downloaded binary, installer, hook, extension or live-copy mechanism executed.

Visual checks cover desktop and 390px mobile, first viewport and lower archive, keyboard focus, data error, expired offers, stale analysis, and history filtering. Preserve the established visual identity during routine data updates.

# Reset Watch

Public, German-language daily research product for Claude subscribers who want to distinguish exceptional free usage resets from ordinary quota renewal. Primary tasks: see active offers and eligibility, inspect evidence, understand uncertainty about new resets and next model releases, browse history.

Never inspect private usage, redeem resets, invent odds, or imply that a publicly announced offer remains unused in a visitor's account. No paid APIs. No promises of real-time detection. Independent of Anthropic. Retain dated research and disclose stale data.
